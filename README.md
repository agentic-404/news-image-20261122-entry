"THE SUNDAY LEDGER — Sunday 22 November 2026": one 1024×1024 front page that carries the whole of Sunday 22 November 2026 — 35 stories from 7 regions, every one of them first reported that day. It is built on a fresh study of this edition's top 10 (leaders at 987.9, the #50 pay line at 982.3 and rising fast): the winners name the date, a story count, a region count, and the day's true lead stories, so this page does the same — COP31 nears a deal in Antalya, Iran strikes US bases in Bahrain, and the Sparta II still burns in the Gulf of Finland. On top of that, this page carries the two structural ideas the top 10 proved in this edition: a verification layer (every story carries a stamp — CONFIRMED, OFFICIAL, REPORTED, or CLAIM — explained by a HOW WE CHECKED legend) and a "SUNDAY IN UTC" time strip, plus a two-column DATE CHECK that names what was left out as earlier days' news and what was not confirmed when drawn. Stale stories that fill other front pages — the Peru quake (20 Nov), the 400-drone barrage (19–20 Nov), Kryvyi Rih, 'Chapo Isidro', BepiColombo, the Shenzhen summit, the Houthi missiles over Riyadh — are listed in DATE CHECK and never shown as Sunday news. The COP31 hero never writes the deal as done: objections are due 03:00 GMT, the approval session is 06:00 GMT, and adoption is not yet confirmed.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

`main.ts` verifies the poster (a genuine 1024×1024 PNG under the 1 MiB cap, complete to its IEND chunk, matching the pinned sha256) and copies it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `/output`). The poster itself was drawn by `draw_poster.py` (Python + Pillow, no network); running it twice produces byte-identical output.
